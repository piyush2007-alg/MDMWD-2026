College Department FAQ

Course registration can be completed through the student portal during the registration period.

You can contact the department office by email or visit during official working hours.

The examination timetable is published on the college website and student portal.

The department office is open from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Scholarship applications can be submitted through the student portal or financial aid office.

Study materials are available on the department learning management system.

Submit a certificate request form to the department office or through the student portal.

Use the “Forgot Password” option on the student portal or contact technical support.

Assignments should be submitted through the learning management system before the deadline.

The academic calendar is available on the college website and student portal.

Contact the department office or club coordinator to learn about membership and registration.

Students can access computer laboratories, libraries, study areas, and departmental resources.
